Privacy Policy

Kalender Hub · last updated 22 November 2026

Who uses the app

Kalender Hub is a private, single-user desktop application. It runs only on its owner's own computer and is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data the app accesses

  • Google Calendar (calendar scope): to show the owner's events and to let the owner create, edit or delete their own events from within the app.
  • Gmail (gmail.readonly scope): read-only, to show the sender, subject and a short preview of the owner's recent inbox messages. The app never sends, deletes or modifies email.

How the data is stored and used

  • All data is processed locally on the owner's computer. OAuth tokens are stored in a local file on that computer only.
  • Calendar and email content is fetched on demand for display and is not stored, sold, shared, or transferred to any third party or server.
  • Google user data is not used for advertising, and is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Limited Use

Kalender Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from the app ("lahuta") or from Google Account → Third-party connections. Deleting the app's local data folder removes all stored tokens.
