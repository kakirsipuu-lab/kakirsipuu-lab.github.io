Kalender Hub

Kalender Hub is a personal desktop application that runs only on its owner's own computer. It combines the owner's calendars (Google Calendar, school timetables and other iCal feeds), unread email headlines, news and weather into a single daily overview.

It is a private, single-user tool. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts, servers or cloud storage of its own.

Privacy Policy

Kalender Hub on isiklik töölauarakendus, mis töötab ainult omaniku arvutis ja koondab tema kalendrid, kirjad, uudised ja ilma üheks päevaülevaateks.
